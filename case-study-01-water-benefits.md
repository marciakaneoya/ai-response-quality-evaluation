# Case Study 01 — AI Response Quality Evaluation

## 1. User Prompt

Explain three benefits of drinking water regularly. Keep your answer simple and suitable for a 10-year-old child.

## 2. Responses Evaluated

### Response A

Drinking water regularly helps your body stay hydrated, supports digestion, and helps regulate body temperature. It can also help you concentrate and maintain normal physical performance. Drinking enough water throughout the day is important for your overall health.

### Response B

Water is important because it cures headaches, prevents all diseases, and makes you smarter. You should drink at least 5 liters every day to stay healthy. Water also replaces the need for food because it gives your body everything it needs.

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

Each response was evaluated using five criteria, with scores ranging from 1 to 5.

| Criterion | Response A | Response B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accuracy | 5 | 1 |
| Relevance | 5 | 2 |
| Clarity | 5 | 3 |
| Instruction Following | 5 | 2 |
| Completeness | 4 | 2 |
| **Total** | **24/25** | **10/25** |

## 4. Evaluation Analysis

### Preferred Response: A

Response A is the better choice because it provides clear, accurate, and relevant information in simple language appropriate for a 10-year-old child.

It addresses the user's request and explains three benefits of drinking water without unnecessary complexity.

Response B contains several serious factual inaccuracies. It incorrectly claims that water cures headaches, prevents all diseases, and can replace food. It also recommends drinking at least five liters daily as a general rule, which is not appropriate for everyone.

## 5. Final Decision

Response A demonstrates significantly better accuracy, relevance, and instruction following.

Response B contains misleading health information and fails to provide a reliable answer.
