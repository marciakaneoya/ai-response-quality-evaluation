# Case Study 03 — Detecting Errors in Data Analysis

## 1. User Prompt

Analyze the following e-commerce data and write a short summary of the store's performance. Identify trends, compare conversion rates, and avoid making assumptions that are not supported by the data.

## 2. Data Provided

| Metric | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Website visits | 1,000 | 1,200 | 1,100 |
| Orders | 50 | 60 | 44 |
| Conversion rate | 5% | 5% | 4% |

## 3. Responses Evaluated

### Response A

The store experienced growth in Week 2, with website visits increasing from 1,000 to 1,200 and orders rising from 50 to 60. The conversion rate remained stable at 5%.

In Week 3, visits decreased slightly to 1,100, while orders dropped to 44. The conversion rate also decreased to 4%.

The data shows a decline in performance during Week 3, but it does not establish the reason for this decline.

### Response B

The store showed consistent growth throughout the three weeks. Website visits increased overall, and the number of orders reached its highest level in Week 3.

The conversion rate remained stable at approximately 5%, indicating that the store maintained consistent performance. The increase in traffic was responsible for the improvement in sales.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

Scores range from 1 to 5 for each criterion.

| Criterion | Response A | Response B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accuracy | 5 | 1 |
| Relevance | 5 | 3 |
| Clarity | 5 | 4 |
| Instruction Following | 5 | 1 |
| Completeness | 5 | 2 |
| **Total** | **25/25** | **11/25** |

## 5. Evaluation Analysis

### Preferred Response: A

Response A accurately interprets the data and identifies the increase in website visits and orders during Week 2, followed by a decline in Week 3.

It correctly identifies the conversion rate reduction from 5% to 4% and avoids making unsupported assumptions about the reasons behind the decline.

Response B contains multiple factual errors. It incorrectly claims that the store experienced consistent growth and that orders reached their highest level in Week 3, when the actual number was 44, compared to 60 in Week 2.

It also incorrectly states that the conversion rate remained stable at approximately 5%, ignoring the decrease to 4%.

Additionally, Response B attributes sales improvement to increased traffic without sufficient evidence.

## 6. Final Decision

Response A is clearly preferred because it demonstrates stronger factual accuracy, logical consistency, and adherence to the user's instructions.

Response B fails to interpret the numerical data correctly and makes unsupported conclusions.
