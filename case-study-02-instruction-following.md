# Case Study 02 — Instruction Following Evaluation

## 1. User Prompt

Write a short email to a customer apologizing for a delayed order. Keep it friendly and professional. Do not offer a refund or discount. Maximum 80 words.

## 2. Responses Evaluated

### Response A

Subject: Apology for Your Delayed Order

Hi Sarah,

We sincerely apologize for the delay in delivering your order. We understand how frustrating this can be and appreciate your patience.

Your order is being processed, and we will share an update as soon as possible.

Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,
Customer Support Team

### Response B

Subject: Sorry for the Delay!

Hi Sarah,

We're so sorry your order is late! We know waiting is frustrating. To make things right, we'd love to offer you a 15% discount on your next purchase.

Your order should arrive soon. Thanks for being such a wonderful customer!

Best,
Customer Support

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

Each response was evaluated using five criteria, with scores ranging from 1 to 5.

| Criterion | Response A | Response B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accuracy | 4 | 4 |
| Relevance | 5 | 5 |
| Clarity | 5 | 5 |
| Instruction Following | 5 | 2 |
| Completeness | 5 | 5 |
| **Total** | **24/25** | **21/25** |

## 4. Evaluation Analysis

### Preferred Response: A

Response A is the better choice because it follows the user's main instructions. It uses a friendly and professional tone, stays within the 80-word limit, and does not offer a refund or discount.

Response B also stays within the word limit, but it violates an explicit instruction by offering a 15% discount. Its tone is more informal than requested. Additionally, it states that the order should arrive soon without any confirmed delivery information. Although the response is relevant and complete as an email, these issues reduce its overall quality.

Response A also has a minor accuracy issue: it claims that the order is being processed, although this information was not provided in the prompt.

## 5. Final Decision

Response A is preferred because it demonstrates stronger instruction following and better alignment with the requested professional tone.

However, its unsupported statement about order processing should be improved.
